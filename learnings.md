# Betting Learnings & Insights

This file tracks key learnings from betting sessions, updated after each results review. Follows the principles in `norsktipping_ai_betting_guide_v2.md`.

## May 25, 2026 Session

**Key Observations:**
- Late goals continue to be a high-variance killer (Sandefjord 1-1 after 92' goal). Strong recommendation to default to **DNB (Draw No Bet)** on home teams in open/volatile matches where draw is heavily forecasted.
- **Handicap bets (-1)** failed again on a draw (Tromsø 1-1). Per guide, these have higher variance — only use when edge >8-10%.
- **Over 2.5 Goals** lost in a low-scoring surprise (HamKam 2-0). Reinforces preference for **Under 2.5** in playoff/tense fixtures.
- Combos died on single legs. Future systems should require every leg to have ≥6% edge + consider safer variants (DNB/Double Chance) on risky legs.
- Winners were mostly strong favorites/home edges (Qadsia, St Mirren, Strømsgodset, Wolfsburg). Value calculation (true prob × 1.06) worked well here.
- New Norwegian matches appeared (HamKam, Strømsgodset, Tromsø). Need consistent odds input/scraping process.

**Action Items for Strategy:**
- Increase DNB usage in combos/systems.
- Add a "late-goal risk flag" in future analysis.
- Review RL_Projects for potential simulation of DNB vs full win probabilities.
